## Rules

- "Free download" is not a license.
- Prefer CC0 or clearly documented commercial-use licenses.
- Never use assets copied from Google Images, Pinterest, random mirrors, or unknown re-upload sites.
- Paid assets require owner approval before purchase.
- Preserve proof of license/source in this document.
- Do not redistribute paid/raw marketplace assets outside the rights granted by their license.
